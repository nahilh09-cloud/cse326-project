About OPTIVUE

Understanding how eyewear frames and lens indexes work together helps you choose glasses that look great and feel comfortable all day.

01

What Is OPTIVUE?

OPTIVUE is an interactive eyewear frame selector and educational prescription lens guide. It helps first-time and experienced glasses wearers explore classic frame styles while learning which lens thickness suits their vision needs.

02

Why Frame Selection Matters

A good frame is more than fashion. Frame shape, bridge width, and material (lightweight titanium or durable acetate) affect facial comfort, lens alignment with your pupillary distance (PD), and how thick lens edges appear.

03

How Lens Indexes Work

A lens index measures how efficiently a lens material bends light. Higher-index materials (like 1.60, 1.67, and 1.74) bend light more sharply, allowing the same prescription power to be crafted into a much thinner and lighter lens.

04

How OPTIVUE Helps Customers

Instead of guessing technical terms at an optical store, customers can browse frames by shape and material, enter their SPH, CYL, Axis, and PD values, and arrive at their optician well-informed and confident.
